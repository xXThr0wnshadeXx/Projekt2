# Outreach drafts (issue #6)

Twelve individual messages, one per person. Each asks for a 20-minute
conversation about **the last time they dealt with math materials**. They do not
pitch Glance (Mom Test: ask about their past, `BUSINESS.md` section 0 and 4).

## Before you send any of these

- **Edit each one for the person.** Replace every `[placeholder]`. Add one
  true sentence about why you are writing to them specifically. A message you
  could send to anyone is a list blast, and `DESIGN.md` section 23 rules those out.
- No real names or emails are kept in this repo. Keep contact details in your
  own notes, and log the send in the tracker below by number and audience only.
- Say it is a student hackathon project. Say you are not selling anything.
- Do not mention disability in a way that assumes it. Use their role or
  what they told you in public.
- Acceptance for #6: **at least 10 sent and at least 2 sessions booked**.
  Counting is done in the tracker at the bottom.
