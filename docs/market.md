## First-year planning

Paid campus conversions × validated annual price = first-year annualized
license revenue. One paid conversion at $1,500 would represent $1,500. Three
would represent $4,500. Both counts and the price are illustrative assumptions.
The first buyer conversation should establish current alternative-media costs,
who owns the budget, and what would justify moving from a free pilot to a paid
license. A free pilot contributes $0 to license revenue.
