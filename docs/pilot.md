## What Glance is

Glance lets blind and low-vision students understand a page of maths by voice,
on their own. It reads the page, helps them grasp its shape and parts, and
writes down their work as they say or type it. It never does the work for
them: no answers, no hints, no next steps.

It works on the materials courses really use (photos, scans, slides and PDFs)
and runs in a browser, with a screen reader or without one.

Try it: https://glance-shreev2703.vercel.app

## Who does what

- **Glance:** sets each student up, checks in weekly, fixes what breaks, and
  reports the four measures at the end of term.
- **The office or instructor:** shares the course's maths materials, and tells
  us what worked and what didn't.
- **Students:** use Glance on real coursework and tell us where it failed them.
  Taking part is their choice and they can stop at any time.

## What happens at the end

If the measures are met, we send a proposal for an annual campus licence, free
for students. If they are not, we report why and nobody owes anything.

## What we can show today, and what we can't

Measured on November 15, 2026 (`eval/notes.md`):

- On 30 computer-generated worksheet pages, 57 of 60 lines were read
  correctly (95%).
- A typed page was read in a median of 1.3 seconds.

Not measured yet, and we will say so if asked:

- Accuracy on real photos and on handwriting.
- Time saved for an office, and cost per page.
- Results with students. No student has used Glance in a course yet.

## Privacy and integrity

- Glance has no accounts and no database, and stores no images, audio or
  student work on its server. Images and audio are sent to the OpenAI API to
  be read, and Glance does not keep them.
- It describes what is on the page and never what to do next, so it does the
  job of a human reader and scribe.
